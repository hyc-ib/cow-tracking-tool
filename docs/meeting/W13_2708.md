  * Scanning performance, throughput, error breakdown statistics across datasets.

* **Chapter 7: Conclusions & Future Work**

## 3. Experimental Evaluation & Visualizations
* **Quantitative Metrics:**
  * Dataset scanning runtime and processing throughput.
  * Comprehensive breakdown and occurrence rates of detected anomalies across multi-hour video feeds.
* **Dual Error Characterization:**
  * **Hard Failures:** Complete tracking errors correctly flagged (e.g., identity swaps, extreme position jumps).
  * **Soft Failures / Edge Cases:** Legitimate cow behaviors flagged due to rapid turns or camera perspective changes, demonstrating the necessity of the human-in-the-loop review interface.
* **Trajectory Interpolation Plots:**
  * Produce 5-variable time-series comparison plots showing sparse ground truth points (e.g., $1\text{ Hz}$) vs. dense interpolated curves $(x, y, w, h, \theta)$.
