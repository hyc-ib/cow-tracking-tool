## 3. Required Data, Statistics & Experimental Evaluation
The dissertation must demonstrate scalability across large-scale video data (tens of hours) rather than isolated examples:
* **Quantitative Statistics:**
  * Total counts and frequency distributions of flagged anomalies across full-length sessions.
  * Percentage of frames/cows impacted by each anomaly rule.
* **Case Study Analysis:**
  * **Success Cases:** Highlight true tracking failures correctly captured by the rules (ID swaps, sudden jumps).
  * **Failure / Edge Cases:** Document false positives (e.g., rapid turning, perspective stretching, occlusions) and analyze underlying causes.
* **Formal Formulations:** Provide mathematical equations for centroid distances, velocity vectors, and geometric area/angle changes.
* **Technical Justifications:** Justify all software design decisions, framework choices (PyQt6, OpenCV, NumPy), and library versions.
